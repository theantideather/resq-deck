One practitioner, eight jobs, four minutes

In most Indian clinics a single dentist or physician works with one assistant. When a patient collapses mid-procedure, everything on the right has to happen at once, from memory, under stress.

Textbooks, refresher courses and protocol charts all assume calm recall. Emergencies do not supply it.

ResQ takes every job except the two that need human hands.

Recall the right protocol and sequence

Council. Runs the whole time, never in the way

Specialist agents read the case, argue it out, and return a ranked set of possibilities plus anything they refuse to rule out.

Speed and accuracy are normally a trade-off. ResQ runs both, and merges them only where it is safe to.

A spectrum, not a verdict

Each agent reasons from one specialty: cardiology, pharmacology, toxicology, oral surgery. They read each other's conclusions and push back before anything is ranked.

all three shares
